# Legal Metrology System - Project Rules

## Design System Guidelines
- Palette: Primary Slate/Navy (#1E293B), Background #F8FAFC, Card Surface #FFFFFF, Text #0F172A.
- Accents: Emerald Green (Verified/Pass), Amber (Due/Pending), Red (Expired/Miscalibrated).
- UI Elements: Crisp 1px borders (border-slate-200), minimal shadows (shadow-sm), clean sans-serif typography.
- STRICT BAN: Zero purple/pink gradients, zero glassmorphism, zero dark-mode glowing effects.

## Domain Terms & Formulas
- LMO: Legal Metrology Officer | GATC: Government Approved Test Centre.
- MPE: Maximum Permissible Error calculation based on Legal Metrology Rules, 2011 (Class I, II, III, IV scales).
- Dynamic QR: HMAC-signed URL string linking directly to public certificate verification.
